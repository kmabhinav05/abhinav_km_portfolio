### 🚀 About Me

I am an analytical problem-solver and aspiring **Data Analyst** with a strong foundation in Computer Applications (BCA) from University of Calicut and active professional training at Techno Dot Academy of R&D&I. Rather than looking at numbers in isolation, I bridge technical data pipelines with business strategy—transforming complex datasets into clear, interactive visualizations, automated dashboards, and actionable insights.

* 💡 **My Approach**: Data Extraction & Cleansing $\rightarrow$ Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) $\rightarrow$ Relational / Star-Schema Modeling $\rightarrow$ Interactive Dashboards & Business Storytelling.
* 🔭 **Currently Focusing**: Advanced DAX calculations, high-performance PostgreSQL queries, automated data transformations, and AI-assisted analytics workflows.
* 🤝 **Open To**: Entry-level Data Analyst roles, Business Intelligence opportunities, data visualization projects, and collaborative SQL query optimization.
